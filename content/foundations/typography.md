Use typography roles consistently rather than adjusting type by eye. The product uses three families for distinct content contexts:

- **Tiempos Headline** for article titles, page headlines, and hero copy.
- **Charter** for long-form body copy in the article composer.
- **Inter** for application UI, including navigation, controls, inputs, labels, and metadata.

## Type roles

- **Display heading**: hero, onboarding, and large empty-state headings.
- **Form heading**: grouped-field and multi-step-flow titles.
- **Heading**: section, card, sidebar-group, dialog, drawer, and panel titles.
- **Body**: paragraphs, stacked descriptions, drawer and dialog copy, and supporting explanations.
- **Label**: field labels, legends, captions, compact list/table headers, and metadata beside titles.
- **Small**: helper text, validation text, timestamps, footnotes, and secondary notes.
- **Extra small**: badges, tags, status pills, and dense table metadata.

Keep hierarchy deliberate: use a heading to introduce a surface or group, a label to identify a control, and body text to explain a task. Preserve the role's existing family, size, weight, and line-height instead of inventing one-off variants.
